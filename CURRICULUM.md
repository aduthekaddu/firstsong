# Firstsong Curriculum

From your first chord to your first original song — six weeks, free.

Derived from market/pedagogy research (see RESEARCH.md on gateway-vn: ~/guitar-research/). Optimized for: adult beginners with a guitar and a phone, one recorded artifact per week, no account required to start.

## Stage 0 — First session (15 minutes)
A personal hook they can play back.
- Tune with a guide; sound an open string; try Em; tap four beats
- Say one line about one concrete memory; hum it in two rhythms; keep one
- Record chord + line (separately is fine)
**Keep:** a 10–20 second personal hook

## Week 1 — A repeatable hook
- Relax the fretting hand; four slow downstrokes
- Four sensory details about an object; one becomes the title line
- Melody on two or three notes
**Keep:** a four-bar memo + written lyric · *check:* repeatable tomorrow, knows the key word

## Week 2 — Accompany a verse
- Second chord (e.g. Asus2); change in isolation; full bars
- Speak a four-line scene in time; mark stressed words; leave room to breathe
**Keep:** eight-bar verse + chord sheet · *check:* one clean change, pulse kept

## Week 3 — A chorus that sounds different
- Higher resting note / longer title note / fuller strum — pick one
- Compare two rhymes by ear; replace one vague phrase with verse detail
**Keep:** verse + chorus a listener can tell apart · *first personal checkpoint*

## Week 4 — Finish the draft
- Form: V C V C (bridge optional); verse two changes time/place/perspective; keep the hook
- Count-in and an intentional ending
**Keep:** start-to-finish take + one-page chord/lyric sheet

## Week 5 — Arrange and revise
- Dynamics map (where strumming lightens/stops); comfortable tempo and singing range
- Record two chorus versions; adjust one thing after listening
**Keep:** revision pair + arrangement map; both takes saved

## Week 6 — Record and export
- Quiet room; level test; count in; full take; trim; separate passes if voice/guitar clash
**Keep:** playable audio file + lyric/chord sheet + backup

## Optional weeks 7–8 — Layers and mix
- DAW: guide memo → rhythm guitar → vocal (headphones) → one harmony max
- Balance, noise removal, export listening copy + master; start song two

## Teaching responses to stalls
- Buzzing/late changes → close-up correction or single bass note
- Blank page → place, object, one sentence they would actually say
- Melody fights language → clap sentence stress, drop a syllable, move the beat
- Cannot sing+strum → record accompaniment first
- Perfectionism → two takes, then listen
- Missed a week → resume from their last audio file, one small task

## Success measures (all learners count, including private submissions)
- Week-one memos and finished demos as fractions of ALL starters
- True-beginner completion tracked separately
- Second song started unaided within 30 days
